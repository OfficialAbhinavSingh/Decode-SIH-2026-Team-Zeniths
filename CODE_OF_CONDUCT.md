# Code of conduct

Six people, one deadline. This is the whole code.

## How we work together

- **Assume good faith.** Nobody breaks the build on purpose.
- **Critique the code, never the person.** "This drops the missing-signal case" — good.
  "You always write sloppy code" — not acceptable here.
- **Say when you're blocked.** Within 2 hours, in the group. A silent blocker is the most expensive
  thing that can happen to a two-week project. Nobody will think less of you. We will think less of
  finding out on 4 September.
- **Say when you can't deliver.** If your load changed — exams, illness, anything — say it early so
  the lane gets reassigned while there's still time. Dropping a lane quietly at the deadline is the
  one thing that actually sinks the team.
- **Give real reviews.** "looks good" on a 300-line diff isn't a review. Read it or say you can't
  get to it today.
- **Take review comments as help, not attack.** Someone read your code carefully. That's a gift.
- **Credit honestly.** Say who did what, including which parts an AI agent wrote (CONTRIBUTING §7).
  We present as a team; the team knows who built what.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind. Sharing a teammate's personal
information. Sharing citizen data from the system outside the team. Deliberately breaking someone
else's lane or bypassing the review rules to get your change in.

## If something goes wrong

Raise it with @OfficialAbhinavSingh. If it involves him, raise it with the team together.
We fix it and keep shipping.
